📖 What the terms mean
Standard cost (We Pay)
The cost currently recorded on the product — what our prices are built on today.
Best offer
The lowest price any available supplier is quoting for that product right now.
Purchase batch
One stock purchase: which supplier, how many units, at what price. Sales consume batches oldest-first.
Extra paid (variance)
The difference when we bought above the cheapest available offer. It is allowed — but recorded, explained and visible here.
Estimated saving / month
Saving per unit × units actually sold in the last 30 days. Theory turned into money.
Profit they made us
For stock bought from this supplier and already sold: customer price minus that stock's real cost.
