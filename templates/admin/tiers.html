ℹ️ How Tier Assignment Works
When a Sales Manager registers a new reseller, they enter:
  • Expected Monthly Sales (SAR) — How much the reseller is expected to spend per month
The system automatically assigns the highest qualifying tier based on the expected monthly sales target. The reseller then sees prices calculated as:
Reseller Price = Default Price − (OC Margin × Tier's Margin Share %)
