ℹ️ How Tier Assignment Works
When a Sales Manager registers a new reseller, they enter:
  • Expected Monthly Sales (SAR) — How much the reseller is expected to spend per month
  • Target Merchants — Which merchants they'll purchase from (STC, PlayStation, Xbox, etc.)
The system automatically assigns the highest qualifying tier — the tier where both the monthly sales target AND minimum merchants are met. The reseller then sees prices calculated as:
Reseller Price = Default Price − (OC Margin × Tier's Margin Share %)
